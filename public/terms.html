3. Where the numbers come from

  • YouTube numbers are the channel’s official statistics from YouTube API Services, checked at most about once a minute.
  • Bluesky numbers come from Bluesky’s public profile service, and Instagram numbers from Instagram’s official API using the account owner’s permission.

We show the numbers as these services provide them. They can be a few minutes old, rounded (for example, YouTube rounds subscriber counts), or briefly unavailable, and they may differ from what you see in each platform’s own apps. For Bluesky and Instagram counters, the site may also show the change over the last 24 hours, a chart, and an average pace (marked “≈”), all worked out from those readings.
