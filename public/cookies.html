Cookie policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This page explains what this website (run by the site owner, contact noobvexbusiness@gmail.com) stores on your device, and the optional features you can allow or refuse. It follows the UK’s Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

In short: no advertising or tracking cookies. The site stores only a few small items that it needs to work or that remember choices you made. Fonts from Google and visit counting run only if you allow them.

1. What the site stores on your device

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. “Local storage” is a similar feature that keeps small pieces of information in your browser; unlike cookies, it isn’t sent to the website automatically. This site uses one cookie, and only for the site owner. Everything else is in local storage and never leaves your device.

NameTypeWhat it’s forHow longConsent needed?
lvc_owner Cookie (this site only) Keeps the site owner signed in. It’s only set when the owner signs in with the site’s password, so visitors never get it. 7 days, or until the owner signs out No: strictly necessary for signing in
lvc.consent Local storage Remembers your privacy choices, so you aren’t asked on every visit. Until you clear it; we ask again after 12 months No: strictly necessary to record your choices
lvc.sound Local storage Remembers whether you turned the tick sound on or off. Only saved once you switch it. Until you clear it No: only records a choice you made
lvc.tab, lvc.skipSetup Local storage Site owner only: remembers which Settings tab was open, and whether the owner skipped connecting YouTube. Until cleared No: only records choices the owner made

2. Optional features (only with your permission)

These don’t store anything on your device, but they do involve information leaving your browser, so the site asks first. Both are off until you allow them.

FeatureProvided byWhat happens
Fonts from Google Google (Google Fonts) Your browser downloads the counter’s typeface from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, which gives Google your IP address and browser details. If you refuse, the counter uses a similar font from your device.
Visit counting Vercel Web Analytics Only offered if the site owner has turned it on. See the Privacy policy for more about how this information is used.

3. No third-party embeds or trackers

The site has no adverts, social media buttons, tracking pixels or embedded videos. Profile pictures in the counters are passed through the site’s own server, so your browser doesn’t contact YouTube, Bluesky or Instagram unless you click a link to them.

4. Changing your choices

  • Use Cookie settings at the bottom of the main page to allow or refuse each optional feature at any time. If you take a permission back, the page reloads so it stops straight away.
  • You can also delete everything the site has stored by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. You’ll then be asked for your choices again.

5. Changes to this policy

If we change what the site stores or the optional features it offers, we’ll update this page and the date at the top, and ask for your choices again where needed.
