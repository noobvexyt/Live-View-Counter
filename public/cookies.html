Cookie policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

In short

  • No advertising or tracking cookies.
  • One cookie keeps you signed in, and a few small settings are saved on your device so the site works the way you set it. These are strictly necessary, so they don’t need your consent.
  • Fonts from Google and visit counting are optional and off unless you allow them.

What this policy covers

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Websites can also save information on your device in other ways, such as your browser’s “local storage”. UK law (the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, or PECR) says we must tell you about all of these and ask your permission, unless something is strictly necessary to give you the service you asked for. This policy lists everything the site saves on your device, and the optional extras that need your permission.

Strictly necessary: cookies

NameWhat it’s forHow long
lvc_session Keeps you signed in to your account. Set only when you sign in or sign up. It holds your username, a session number, when it ends, and a signature so it can’t be forged. Scripts can’t read it, and it isn’t sent with requests from other websites. Until you close your browser (at most 12 hours), or 30 days if you tick “Keep me signed in”. Ends when you sign out.
lvc_admin Only for the person who runs the site: keeps them signed in to the site owner’s tools. 12 hours, or until they sign out of those tools

Strictly necessary: saved on your device (local storage)

These stay on your device. They aren’t sent to us or anyone else.

NameWhat it’s forHow long

Optional: only if you allow them

The first time you open a counter page, or if visit counting is turned on, the site asks for your choices. Both options start switched off, and “Reject all” is as easy as “Accept all”.

WhatWhoWhat happens
Fonts from Google Google On counter pages, your browser downloads typefaces from Google Fonts, which gives Google your IP address and browser details. Google says Google Fonts doesn’t set cookies. Without this, counters use a similar font from your device. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
Visit counting (only offered if the site has it turned on) Vercel Web Analytics Records the pages you view, the site you came from, your country and your type of device, browser and operating system, so we can see how many visits the site gets. It uses no cookies and saves nothing on your device. See the Privacy policy.

Changing your choices

Use Cookie settings at the bottom of any page to change your choices at any time. Taking permission back reloads the page so nothing optional keeps running. You can also delete cookies and saved site data in your browser’s settings. If you delete lvc_session you’ll be signed out, and if you delete lvc.consent we’ll ask for your choices again.
