Who can see your page

A public page shows your counters, your channel’s name and picture, and your username (it’s in the address). Anyone who has the address can see it, so think about who you share it with. You need a public page to use it in OBS. After you make it private, copies kept by our hosting provider can take up to 2 minutes to disappear.
