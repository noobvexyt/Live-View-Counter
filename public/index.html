Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. It’s free, with no adverts and no paid extras.

How often do the numbers change?

YouTube numbers are checked about once a minute, and your page shows them as soon as they arrive. YouTube itself only updates channel totals every so often, and it rounds subscriber counts, so the numbers can sit still for a while and then jump.

Are these the real numbers?

Yes. YouTube numbers come straight from YouTube’s official API and Bluesky numbers from Bluesky’s public API. Nothing is guessed or estimated.

Can other people see my page?

Only if you make it public on your account page. A public page can be seen by anyone who has its address. You can make it private again at any time.

How do I use it in OBS?

Make your page public, then open your page’s settings and go to Stream. Copy the address shown there into a Browser source in OBS.

What if I forget my password?

When you sign up you get a recovery code. Use it on the forgotten password page to choose a new password. Keep it somewhere safe: we don’t have your email, so it’s the only way back in.

Is this made by YouTube or Bluesky?

No. Live View Counter is an independent site and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube, Google or Bluesky.
