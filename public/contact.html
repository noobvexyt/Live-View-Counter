Contact and reports

Live View Counter is run by NoobVex, an individual in the United Kingdom.

Email: noobvexbusiness@gmail.com

We aim to reply within 7 days. Reports about harmful content or a child’s safety are looked at first.

Report a page

If a page on this site breaks our Terms, is illegal, or could harm someone (especially a child), tell us. You don’t need an account. Email us with:

  • the page’s address (it looks like /u/username),
  • what’s wrong with it, and
  • if you can, when you saw it.

Email a report

What happens next: we look at the page, and if it breaks our Terms or the law we can hide it, suspend the account or delete it. We’ll tell you what we decided if you ask. If someone is in immediate danger, call 999 (in the UK) first.

Because pages on this site only show public numbers from YouTube and Bluesky, a channel name or picture you’re worried about will usually also need reporting to YouTube or Bluesky. You can do that on their own sites.

Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we dealt with a report, with a decision about your account (like a suspension), or with anything else about this site, email us with “Complaint” in the subject. Tell us what happened and what you’d like us to do. We’ll look at it again, fairly, and reply within 14 days. If your account was suspended or deleted and you think that was wrong, you can appeal the same way.

Your data and your rights

Most things you can do yourself, straight away, on your account page: download your data, change your password, make your page private, or delete your account. For anything else, such as a question about your data or a request under UK data protection law, email us. We may ask you to prove the account is yours, for example by signing in. See the Privacy policy for your rights.

If you’re not happy with how we handle your personal data, you can complain to the UK’s Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113. We’d appreciate the chance to sort it out first.

Security problems

If you find a security problem with this site, please email us privately rather than posting about it, and don’t access other people’s accounts. Thank you.
