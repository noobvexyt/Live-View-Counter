1. Who we are

Live View Counter (the “site”) is run by NoobVex, an individual based in the United Kingdom (“we”, “us”, “our”). We decide how and why personal data is used on the site, so we are the “controller” under UK data protection law: the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Contact us about anything in this policy at noobvexbusiness@gmail.com. There’s more on the Contact page.

2. What we keep, why, and our lawful basis

Your account

When you sign up we keep:

  • your username;
  • your password and recovery code, but only as one-way coded versions (hashes). Nobody, including us, can read them back;
  • when you made the account, last signed in and last changed your password;
  • that you confirmed you’re 13 or older, and which version of the Terms you agreed to and when;
  • whether your page is public or private, and its settings: the counters you add, their labels and goals, and how the page looks and sounds;
  • the YouTube channel you connect (its channel ID, what you typed to find it, and when), and the Bluesky profiles you add;
  • if we suspend your account, when and why.

We use this to run your account and your page. Lawful basis: contract, because we need it to provide the service you asked for. We keep the record of your age confirmation and agreement to the Terms, and any suspension, for our legitimate interests in showing that our rules were agreed to and in keeping the site safe.

Staying signed in

When you sign in, the site sets one cookie, lvc_session, holding your username, a session number, when it ends, and a signature so it can’t be forged. It’s strictly necessary for signing in. See the Cookie policy. Lawful basis: contract.

Protecting accounts from password guessing and abuse

When someone signs in, uses a recovery code, signs up, or uses the site owner’s sign-in, the site keeps a count of tries against a one-way coded version of their IP address (a keyed hash, not the address itself), and counts wrong passwords and recovery codes for each username. It also counts how many channel and profile look-ups each account makes. These counts delete themselves after 15 minutes, or 1 hour for sign-ups and look-ups. Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in keeping accounts secure and the site working for everyone.

When you visit any page

Like any website, when your browser asks for a page our hosting provider, Vercel, receives your IP address, your browser and device details, the pages requested, the time, and the page that linked you here. Vercel needs these to deliver the site and keep it secure, and keeps them in its logs for a limited time. We don’t use them to identify you. Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in running a working, secure website.

Public numbers of the channels and profiles shown

For each connected YouTube channel and added Bluesky profile, the site keeps the latest public information from YouTube or Bluesky: the name, handle, profile picture, link and public counts. One copy is shared by every page that shows that channel or profile, and it isn’t linked to any account. Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in showing public account statistics that the account’s owner, or a user, chose to display.

Reports and moderation

If you report a page or make a complaint, we use what you send us (including your email address) to look into it, act on it and reply. If we suspend an account, we keep the reason with the account. Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in keeping the site safe and lawful, and our legal obligations, such as those under the Online Safety Act 2023.

If you email us

We use your email address, your name if you give it, and what you write to reply to you. Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in answering messages sent to us.

Fonts from Google (only if you allow it)

Counter pages can use typefaces from Google Fonts. If you allow “Fonts from Google” in your privacy choices, your browser downloads them from Google’s servers, which gives Google your IP address and browser details. Google handles this under its own Privacy Policy (see also the Google Fonts privacy FAQ). If you don’t allow it, nothing is sent to Google and counters use a similar font from your device. The site’s other pages never use them. Lawful basis: your consent, which you can withdraw any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of each page.

Visit counting with Vercel Web Analytics (only if it’s on and you allow it)

If the site has visit counting turned on, you’ll be asked first. If you allow it, Vercel Web Analytics records each page you view, the site you came from, your country, and your type of device, operating system and browser. It uses no cookies. According to Vercel, visitors are told apart using a code made from the request, which is thrown away after 24 hours, so we can’t identify you. We only see totals. Lawful basis: your consent, which you can withdraw any time with “Cookie settings”.

We don’t use your data for advertising, we don’t build profiles of you, and the site makes no decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects. (The only automatic decision is a short pause on signing in after too many wrong passwords.)

3. What other people can see

Your page starts private: only you can see it, while signed in. If you make it public on your account page, anyone who has its address (/u/yourusername) can see your username (it’s in the address), your counters, and the names, pictures and public numbers of the channel and profiles on it. Search engines are asked not to list pages. You can make it private again at any time. Copies kept by our hosting provider’s network can take up to 2 minutes to disappear. Nobody else can see your settings, your account details or whether you have an account.

4. YouTube and Bluesky

This site uses YouTube API Services to show public YouTube channel statistics. By using the site to show a YouTube channel, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s use of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

From YouTube, the site reads only a channel’s public details: its ID, name, handle, profile picture, and total views, subscribers and number of videos. It does this with the site’s own API key: you never sign in to Google here, and the site can’t see your Google account, your private data or anything you haven’t made public on YouTube. The latest reading is refreshed about once a minute while a page showing the channel is open. It’s deleted within 7 days once no page asks for it, and readings that can’t be refreshed are deleted within 30 days. To stop the site reading your channel, disconnect it in your page’s settings (App) or delete your account.

From Bluesky, the site reads a profile’s public name, handle, picture and follower and post counts through Bluesky’s public API, in the same way.

All of this is requested by the site’s own server, not your browser, and profile pictures are passed through the site too. So when you view a page, no information about you is sent to YouTube (Google) or Bluesky. If you click a link to their sites, their own privacy policies apply there. The site does store a few small items on your device, as listed in the Cookie policy. None of them are used for YouTube or shared with anyone.

5. Who we share data with

We use these service providers, who handle data for us under contract:

  • Vercel Inc. hosts the site and its network of servers, and provides visit counting if you allow it. See Vercel’s privacy policy.
  • Upstash Inc. provides the site’s database (stored in London), which holds accounts, page settings, the latest public numbers and the short-lived security counts. See Upstash’s privacy policy.

The site’s server also sends channel IDs and Bluesky handles (but nothing about you or your account) to Google (YouTube Data API) and Bluesky to read the public numbers. Google receives your IP address and browser details only if you allow fonts from Google.

We may share information if the law requires it, for example with the police or a regulator such as Ofcom. We don’t sell personal data or use it for advertising.

6. Transfers outside the UK

The site’s database and main servers are in London, but Vercel and Upstash may also process data in the United States and other countries, for example for support, security and their global network. Where data leaves the UK, we rely on the safeguards these providers offer under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where a provider is certified under it.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • have it corrected if it’s wrong, or deleted;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • move data you gave us to another service (data portability);
  • withdraw consent at any time, for fonts from Google or visit counting, with “Cookie settings”. This doesn’t affect anything done before.

You can do the most common things yourself, straight away, on your account page: download your data (a file with everything in your account), change your page’s settings, make your page private, and delete your account. For anything else, email noobvexbusiness@gmail.com. We’ll reply within one month. We may ask you to show the account is yours, for example by signing in, and because we hold so little, we may not be able to link data such as hosting logs to you.

9. Children and young people

You must be 13 or older to make an account. If you’re under 18, please check with a parent or carer that they’re happy for you to use the site. If we find out that someone under 13 has an account, we’ll delete it. Parents and carers can contact us about this.

We follow the ICO’s Age Appropriate Design Code (the Children’s code). The site is designed to be private by default for everyone: we don’t ask for real names, emails, ages or locations; pages start private; we don’t use your data for advertising or profiling; there’s no messaging, comments or sharing between users; and the optional extras (fonts from Google and visit counting) stay off unless you turn them on. Before you make your page public, the site explains who will be able to see it.

10. Security

The site is only served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Passwords and recovery codes are stored only as strong one-way hashes (scrypt and SHA-256). The sign-in cookie can’t be read by scripts or sent from other sites, repeated wrong passwords are blocked for a while, and changing your password signs you out everywhere else. Please use a password you don’t use anywhere else, and keep your recovery code safe. If we ever had a security problem that put your data at risk, we’d deal with it as the law requires, including telling the ICO when needed. As we don’t have your email, we’d tell you on the site.

11. Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your personal data, please tell us first at noobvexbusiness@gmail.com. We’ll acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and respond without undue delay.

You also have the right to complain to the UK’s data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change makes a real difference to how we use your data, we’ll tell you on the site before it takes effect and, where the law needs it, ask for your agreement again.
