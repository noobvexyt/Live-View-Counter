Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

In short

  • You don’t need an account, and the site never asks visitors for personal details.
  • No advertising, no tracking cookies, and we never sell data.
  • Fonts from Google and visit counting (analytics) run only if you allow them, and you can change your mind any time.
  • The site’s own server fetches the numbers it shows from YouTube, Bluesky and Instagram. Your browser doesn’t contact those platforms unless you click a link to them.

1. Who we are

This website (the “site”) is run by the site owner, who is based in the United Kingdom (“we”, “us”, “our”). We decide how and why personal data is used on the site, so we are the “controller” under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Contact us about anything in this policy at noobvexbusiness@gmail.com.

2. What the site does

The site shows public numbers from social media accounts chosen by the site owner, such as a YouTube channel’s total views, subscribers and videos, or a Bluesky or Instagram account’s followers and posts. Only the site owner can sign in to change what it shows.

3. Personal data we use, why, and our lawful basis

When you visit

Like any website, when your browser asks for a page our hosting provider, Vercel, receives your IP address, your browser and device details (the “user agent”), the pages and files requested, the time, and the page that linked you here. Vercel needs these to deliver the site and keep it secure, and keeps them in its logs for a limited time under its own policies. We don’t use them to identify you.

Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in running a working, secure website.

Owner sign-in protection

If someone types a wrong password into the owner sign-in, the site stores a one-way coded version of their IP address (a keyed hash, not the address itself) with a count of failed tries, so it can block password guessing. It is deleted automatically after 15 minutes.

Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in keeping the site secure.

Fonts from Google (only if you allow it)

The counter’s typeface comes from Google Fonts. If you allow “Fonts from Google” in the privacy choices, your browser downloads the fonts from Google’s servers, which gives Google your IP address and browser details. Google handles this under its own Privacy Policy (see also the Google Fonts privacy FAQ). If you don’t allow it, nothing is sent to Google and the site uses a similar font from your device.

Lawful basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the main page.

Visit counting with Vercel Web Analytics (only if you allow it)

If the site owner has turned it on and you allow it, Vercel Web Analytics records each page you view, the site you came from, your country, and your type of device, operating system and browser. It uses no cookies. According to Vercel, visitors are told apart using a code made from the request, which is discarded after 24 hours, so we can’t identify you. We only see totals, such as how many visits the site had. Vercel processes this for us as our service provider.

Lawful basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with “Cookie settings”.

If you email us

We use your email address, your name if you give it, and what you write to reply to you. We keep the conversation for as long as we need it to deal with your message, and delete it within 12 months of our last reply unless we need it longer for a legal reason.

Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in answering messages sent to us.

Accounts shown on the site

The counters show public information about the accounts the site owner chooses: the account’s name, handle, profile picture, link and public counts. This comes from the platforms’ official services (see section 4). We store only the latest numbers and, for Bluesky and Instagram counters, up to 25 hours of readings for the optional chart. YouTube numbers that can’t be refreshed are deleted within 30 days. If a counter shows an account that belongs to you and you want it removed, email us.

Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in showing public account statistics.

The site owner

If you are the site owner: signing in sets one strictly necessary cookie (see the Cookie policy). The YouTube API key and any Instagram access token you add are kept in the site’s private database, are never shown on the page, and are only used to read your accounts’ public numbers. An Instagram token is deleted when you remove its counter, and “Disconnect” deletes the saved YouTube API key.

4. YouTube, Bluesky and Instagram

This site uses YouTube API Services to show public YouTube channel statistics. By using the site you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google’s use of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

The numbers are requested by the site’s own server, not by your browser, and profile pictures are passed through the site’s server too. So no information about you is sent to YouTube (Google), Bluesky or Instagram (Meta) when you view the site. If you click a link to one of their sites, their own privacy policies apply there.

The site doesn’t use YouTube API Services to access any visitor’s YouTube account or private data. The site does store a few small items on your device, such as your privacy choices and whether sound is on, as listed in the Cookie policy. None of them are used for YouTube or shared with anyone.

5. Who we share data with

  • Vercel Inc. hosts the site and, if you allow it, provides the visit counting. See Vercel’s privacy policy.
  • Upstash Inc. provides the site’s database, which holds the site’s settings, the latest numbers, the short chart history, the site owner’s keys, and the 15-minute sign-in protection codes. It holds no information about visitors beyond those codes.
  • Google receives your IP address and browser details only if you allow fonts from Google (see above).

We may also share information if the law requires it. We don’t sell personal data or use it for advertising.

6. Transfers outside the UK

Vercel and Upstash may process data in the United States and in other countries outside the UK. Where data leaves the UK, we rely on the safeguards these providers offer under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where a provider is certified under it.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • have it corrected if it’s wrong, or deleted;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • move data you gave us to another service (data portability);
  • withdraw consent at any time, for fonts from Google or visit counting, with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the main page. This doesn’t affect anything done before you withdrew it.

The site makes no decisions about you by automated means. To use any of these rights, email noobvexbusiness@gmail.com. We’ll reply within one month. Because we hold so little, we may not be able to link data such as hosting logs to you.

9. Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your personal data, please tell us first at noobvexbusiness@gmail.com. We’ll acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and respond without undue delay.

You also have the right to complain to the UK’s data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

10. Children

The site doesn’t ask anyone for personal data, and the optional features (fonts from Google and visit counting) stay off unless someone turns them on. If you’re under 13, please ask a parent or carer before changing the privacy choices.

11. Security

The site is only served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Only the site owner can change anything, after signing in with a password. The sign-in cookie can’t be read by scripts or sent from other websites, and repeated wrong passwords are blocked.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change affects what you’ve agreed to, we’ll ask for your choices again.
